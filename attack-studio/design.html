    How training changes combat

    Strength or Intelligence can scale power; select any stat per attack. Stamina adds 2 maximum HP and 1 energy per level. Speed reduces startup and recovery by up to 15%, keeping the hit window stable. Focus reduces energy cost up to 20%, increases guard reduction from 35% to 55%, and shortens harmful statuses up to 25%. Charisma boosts shields up to 20%.

    No random critical hits, accuracy rolls, or automatic dodges. This makes timing and positioning readable. Slow/haste/weaken display their proposed movement or outgoing-power modifier; this stationary-target test does not simulate AI movement or retaliation.

    Attack settings

    Damage, healing & targeting
    Timing · 60 frames = 1 second

    Startup is the warning; active frames can connect; recovery is the commitment. Cooldown begins on cast. Recast waits for both cooldown and recovery.

    Hitbox · arena pixels

    The orange box decides contact, not the VFX silhouette. Target hurtbox is 28 × 60 px. Melee/area use a fixed rectangle; projectile moves it forward.

    Status & support effects

    Status applies on an unblocked contact. Repeated hits refresh the same status; they do not stack. Burn/poison damage and regeneration heal on each tick. Slow, haste and weaken use a percentage. Stun uses duration.

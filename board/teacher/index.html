Open classroom board ↗

Unlock the desk, then click characters in the preview to select them. Preview positions can differ from the classroom display; saved settings use stable character identities.

What do the board’s top buttons do?
  • Share food: choose a nearby pair to offer a carried snack.
  • Friendships: inspect relationship scores and enable teacher-authorized saving.
  • Teacher rewards: issue a saved participation or confirmed-sharing reward.
  • Teacher controls: select glowing groups, adjust size and floor alignment, control events and emotes.
  • Board settings: refresh the roster, load local ZIP previews, show or hide names.
  • Pause: stop this display’s simulation. Full screen: expand this display.
